The models were simplified and re-aligned to a face-tracking head frame in the
xray-mirror project (github.com/MasterOfVFX2070/xray-mirror). We use that adapted
model data unchanged. Because of the share-alike term, these two data files stay
under CC BY-SA 2.1 JP and anyone may reuse them on the same terms.
